How it works: both phones need Bluetooth on and this app installed. One phone taps Make Me Discoverable, the other taps Find Partner's Phone and taps its name in the list. You pair once in Android, then the apps link.

Honest limits: it works when you are in the same room or house (Bluetooth range, about 10 m), with Bluetooth on, and the app opened recently. If your partner's phone has been locked for a long time, Android may pause listening — open the app once to reconnect.

Private by design: a suggestion never shows its title on the lock screen — the notification only ever says "You have a new idea waiting — open to see".
